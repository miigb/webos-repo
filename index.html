How to install on LG 55UP7700 (webOS 6.0 / 03.53.45):
  1. Open Homebrew Channel on your LG TV.
  2. Install YouTube Cobalt AdFree (webOS 6.0 v1.1.6).
  3. Log in with your primary Google account — all your playlists, history, and subs will work with NO 30s playback cutoff!
